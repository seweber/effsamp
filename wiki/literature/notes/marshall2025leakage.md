# Incoherent approximation of leakage in quantum error correction

[PDF](../pdf/marshall2025leakage.pdf) · [BibTeX](../bib/marshall2025leakage.bib) · <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.23.054025>

## Summary

The paper approximates leakage in quantum error correction by twirling the dynamics into sectors, so that a leaked qudit becomes a classical label and the coherent part stays a qubit state vector. noisecirc implements this approximation for qutrits.

## Relevance

Cited by noisecirc for its subspace twirling, which PLAN.md excludes from this project (qubits only). Recorded so that noisecirc's citation is verified. See [Prior art](../../studies/prior-art.md).
