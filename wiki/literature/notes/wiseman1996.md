# Quantum trajectories and quantum measurement theory

[PDF](../pdf/wiseman1996.pdf) · [BibTeX](../bib/wiseman1996.bib) · <https://doi.org/10.1088/1355-5111/8/1/015>

## Summary

The paper reviews quantum trajectories as conditioned evolution under measurement. One of its methods chooses an outcome with a state-independent ostensible probability, applies the corresponding measurement operator scaled by that probability, and carries the squared norm of the result as a weight, so that the actual probability is the ostensible one times the norm. Linear (unnormalized) quantum trajectories follow from this view.

## Relevance

The theoretical basis of noisecirc's proposals: noisecirc uses ostensible probabilities proportional to the operator norms of the Kraus operators, so that every weight is at most one and can be turned into an acceptance probability.
