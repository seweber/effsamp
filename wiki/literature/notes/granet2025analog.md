# Analog simulation of noisy quantum circuits

[PDF](../pdf/granet2025analog.pdf) · [BibTeX](../bib/granet2025analog.bib) · <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.7.013213>

## Summary

The paper studies unravelings of noisy circuits in which the trajectory operators need not be unitary and the norm of the state changes along the trajectory. Its digital sampling writes each Kraus operator as a state-independent probability times an operator of unit operator norm, and weights trajectories by their norms; for amplitude damping it then proposes analog unravelings that reduce variance.

## Relevance

Uses operator-norm normalisation of Kraus operators with state-independent probabilities, as noisecirc does, but as importance weights rather than acceptance probabilities. See [Prior art](../../studies/prior-art.md).
